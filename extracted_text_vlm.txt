Q.2. What is the difference BOD and COD?

• BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) measure the
amount of oxygen required by microorganisms
to break down organic matter in
water over a specific period (usually
5 days at 20°C). It indicates
the level of organic pollution.

• COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand) measures the
total oxygen required to chemically
oxidize both organic and inorganic
substances in water. It provides
a broader indication of water pollution.

Key difference: BOD reflects biodegradable
organic pollution, while COD measures
total oxygen demand, including both
biodegradable and non-biodegradable substance.
COD values are typically higher than BOD.
